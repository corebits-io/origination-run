# Mandate — worked example

> **Everything on this page is invented.** Kestrel Lane Capital does not exist,
> nor do the two platforms, the senders, or the companies named in section 8. It
> is here to show the level of specificity that makes a 2,000-row run work. The
> sample run in `samples/sample-run.md` is this mandate, start to finish.
>
> Notice what the detail is doing. The named platforms give the add-on verdict a
> test. The licence requirement gives depth 1 a free, verified drop. The sender
> credibility line in section 11 is the only reason the first touch has anything
> true to say.

## 6. Must-haves

| # | Must-have | How a researcher would test it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Holds its own active state electrical contractor licence, issued ten or more years ago | State licensing board roster: licence number, status, original issue date. Tier 1 and free |
| 2 | A named second-tier manager who is neither the owner nor a family member | Team page, state filings listing officers, trade press. If nobody is named anywhere, that is unknown, not a fail |
| 3 | Service, maintenance or contract revenue at or above 40% | The owner saying so on the record, service-agreement language on their own site, or a run of recurring public contract awards |
| 4 | No single customer above 35% of revenue, where it is stated anywhere | Owner statement or trade press. Usually unknown. Unknown stays unknown |

## 9. Timing signals we care about most

| Family | Rank 1-4 | What specifically, for you |
|---|---|---|
| Leadership and succession | 1 | The owner saying on the record that no family member is taking it on. A founder moving to chairman. A long-tenured partner exiting the register |
| Financing | 2 | A new lender of record, a refinancing, a sale-leaseback on the yard or the building |
| Hiring | 3 | A first controller or CFO. A first general manager. Never just a volume of open roles |

## 13. Notes

We have done this badly before. In 2024 we bought a 4,000-row list, emailed
2,800 of them in six weeks from a new domain, got 31 replies and 2 calls, burned
the domain, and had an owner forward our email to one of Calder's largest
customers with the words "is this what you people do now". The number that
matters in section 10 is not the enumeration target. It is the twelve
conversations.

The lender who sees everything in this market is a regional bank in
Chattanooga. Their new facility announcements have put three of our last four
conversations on the table. Treat a new lender of record as a strong financing
signal.
